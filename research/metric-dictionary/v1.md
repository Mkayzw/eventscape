# Metric Dictionary v1

## Scope

This version defines the four terminal metrics used by the first canonical P0 slice. Metrics observe finalized attendance and the network after tie updates.

## `attendanceRate`

$$
\text{attendanceRate}=\frac{\text{number of final attendees}}{\text{number of eligible agents}}
$$

- **Unit:** proportion
- **Range:** $[0,1]$
- **Direction:** contextual
- **Eligible agent:** an available agent with at least one feasible event before ranking
- **Undefined behavior:** null with `NO_ELIGIBLE_AGENTS`
- **Fixture:** 2 attendees among 4 eligible agents gives `0.5`

## `organizerExposureGini`

For exposure counts $x_i$ for all organizers with at least one active event:

$$
G=\frac{\sum_i\sum_j |x_i-x_j|}{2n\sum_i x_i}
$$

- **Unit:** index
- **Range:** $[0,1)$ for finite nonnegative populations
- **Direction:** lower means more equal exposure
- **All-zero behavior:** `0`
- **Undefined behavior:** null with `NO_ACTIVE_ORGANIZERS` when no active organizer exists
- **Fixture:** counts `[4,0]` give `0.5`; counts `[2,2]` give `0`

## `sameGroupEdgeProportion`

$$
\text{sameGroupEdgeProportion}=
\frac{\sum_{(i,j):g_i=g_j} w_{ij}}{\sum_{(i,j)} w_{ij}}
$$

- **Unit:** proportion
- **Range:** $[0,1]$
- **Direction:** lower usually indicates more cross-group mixing, subject to group composition
- **Graph semantics:** undirected edges counted once; weights are included
- **Undefined behavior:** null with `NO_EDGES`
- **Fixture:** one same-group edge of weight `0.25` and one cross-group edge of weight `0.25` gives `0.5`

## `crossGroupTieFormationRate`

$$
\text{crossGroupTieFormationRate}=
\frac{\text{new cross-group edges}}{\text{all new edges}}
$$

- **Unit:** proportion
- **Range:** $[0,1]$
- **Direction:** higher means more newly formed bridging ties
- **Counting rule:** newly created edges count once; strengthening an existing edge does not enter the denominator
- **Undefined behavior:** null with `NO_NEW_EDGES`
- **Fixture:** 2 cross-group edges among 5 new edges give `0.4`
